The NFL

The Structure of Professional Football

Understanding the NFL's conference structure and playoff format is the foundation of any football fan's education heading into 2027.

🏈 AFC (American Football Conference)

  • Teams: 16 teams in 4 divisions
  • Season: September – February
  • Format: 17 regular season games
  • Playoffs: 6 teams per conference qualify
  • Super Bowl: AFC Champion competes

🏈 NFC (National Football Conference)

  • Teams: 16 teams in 4 divisions
  • Season: September – February
  • Format: 17 regular season games
  • Historic powerhouses: Cowboys, 49ers, Packers
  • Super Bowl: NFC Champion competes

🏆 NFL Playoffs

  • Format: 12 teams (6 per conference)
  • Wild Card: 4th-6th seeds compete
  • Divisional Round: 1st-3rd seeds enter
  • Conference Championships: Final two teams per conference
  • Trophy: Lombardi Trophy to Super Bowl winner

🏆 Super Bowl LXI (2027)

  • Date: February 7, 2027
  • Location: TBD
  • Teams: AFC Champion vs. NFC Champion
  • Format: Single game, neutral venue
  • TV audience: 115+ million viewers worldwide
Key metrics

Stats That Tell the Story

Modern football is driven by advanced analytics. Here are the metrics every serious NFL fan should understand.

📊

EPA (Expected Points Added)

EPA measures how many points a play gains or loses for a team based on field position. Positive EPA plays advance a team's scoring probability; negative EPA plays hurt it. It reveals which QBs and play-callers create true value.

🏃

Yards After Contact (YAC)

The number of yards a player gains after being first contacted by a defender. High YAC receivers and running backs generate explosive plays and turn short gains into big ones — the mark of elite offensive talent.

🛡️

Pressure Rate (QB Pressures)

How often a QB is hit or hurried on a play. Elite pass rushes generate high pressure rates, while strong offensive lines keep pressure rates low. Directly impacts QB accuracy and decision-making in crunch moments.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a down in NFL football? +
A down is a single play starting when the ball is snapped from the center. The offense gets four downs to move the ball at least 10 yards forward. If successful, they receive four new downs; if they fail, possession turns over to the defense. Strategic decisions about when to "go for it" or punt are crucial to NFL gameplay.
How do NFL playoffs work? +
Twelve teams make the playoffs: the top 3 teams from each conference plus 3 wild card teams per conference (ranked by record). The wild card round features the 4th-6th seeded teams, and the top 3 seeds get byes into the divisional round. The AFC and NFC champions then meet in Super Bowl LXI on February 7, 2027.
What's the difference between AFC and NFC? +
The AFC (American Football Conference) and NFC (National Football Conference) are the two conferences that make up the NFL, each with 16 teams split into four divisions. During the regular season, teams play primarily within their conference and division. The AFC and NFC champions then face off in the Super Bowl.
How many games are in the regular season? +
Each NFL team plays 17 regular season games (increased from 16 starting in 2021). Teams play games from September through December, with the playoffs starting in January. This gives teams 6 weeks to rest and recover before playoff intensity begins.
What is Super Bowl LXI in 2027? +
Super Bowl LXI (61) is the 2027 NFL championship game scheduled for February 7, 2027. It will be played between the AFC Champion and the NFC Champion at a neutral stadium location. It's the most watched sporting event in America and attracts over 115 million viewers worldwide, making it the ultimate stage for NFL supremacy.
